Visit live site All projects

Project overview

Odipocodero is the home of author Odipo C. Odero: readers can browse his books, buy them with M-Pesa or a bank card, and read their purchases online, alongside a blog, events and a newsletter. An admin area lets the author manage books, posts, comments and purchases.

In 2026 I redesigned the site for speed and polish while keeping its black-and-white identity: build-time styling instead of an in-browser compiler, pages that load on demand, and book covers served resized (up to 40 times smaller), plus a smoother signature animation and a cleaner layout on every device.

Key features

Books & online reader

A clear catalogue with coming-soon titles, and a reader where buyers open their books with a payment reference.

M-Pesa & card checkout

A focused three-step checkout powered by Paystack, recording each purchase for the author.

Fast loading

On-demand page loading and resized covers cut first-visit downloads roughly in half.

Responsive design

A calm, readable layout with a full-screen mobile menu, from phones to large desktops.
